Background: the honest trigger was not raw bandwidth but the missing MAC. On the G4 every new feature had to be squeezed onto the bus already carrying the control loop, so each one made the loop worse. The H5 gave us a second pipe. We are not keeping CAN out of conviction: it is the migration path. Everything ported to Ethernet stays there, and the bus shrinks release after release until only the control loop is left, then that moves too.
